Reasons to Choose Assisted Living

August 8, 2024· Angels Touch team

In the past, senior citizens had limited options when it came to living arrangements in their older years. Today, you can find communities meant for just that. Assisted living communities are growing and more seniors are finding this to be the ideal living arrangement. According to A Place for Mom, 18,800 Americans currently reside in an assisted living community. Let's look at several reasons why you might want to consider an assisted living arrangement.

Daily Living Assistance

Most seniors want a nice balance of assistance and independent living. Living on their own can be challenging and even dangerous, but a full-scale nursing facility can greatly intrude on their independence. Assisted living communities help individuals find the right balance of care.

Within an assisted living community, seniors can get help with daily living challenges. Some of the most common problems that seniors deal with in daily living include personal tasks such as bathing and personal hygiene, bathroom needs, and dressing. They may also need help with shopping, meal prep, and housework.

It's important to research the level of care and assistance each facility is willing to offer, though. Different places will offer different services, so you need to check and make sure that the services being offered match what your loved one needs.

Medical Management

Most elderly people have some level of medical care they require. This could be as simple as taking medication daily, or it could include more involved forms of treatment. It can sometimes be challenging for aging people to manage their own medical care, and this is where assisted living can help.

When seniors forget to take medication or to take other treatments, it can complicate their health and reduce the amount of independence they can enjoy. Assisted living facilities can vary greatly on the amount of medical assistance they provide. Be sure to talk with the facility or community to ensure everyone is clear on what's needed and offered.

These are just a few reasons why people might choose to live in an assisted living community.
