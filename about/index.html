About Angels Touch

Independently owned. Personally run.

Since 2008, Angels Touch has been “a safe place to call home” for families across De Pere, Green Bay and neighboring communities. We’re not a corporate chain — you’ll always deal directly with the owners.

Three neighboring residences, each with twenty suites, on a park-like street in the Town of Ledgeview. Small enough that leadership knows every resident personally — and decisions about care happen down the hall, not at a head office.
