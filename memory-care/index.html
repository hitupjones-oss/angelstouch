Memory care · Green Bay & De Pere

Care that remembers who they are.

Memory care is the cornerstone of Angels Touch. For residents living with Alzheimer’s or dementia, we pair calm, familiar routines with caregivers trained every month in dementia care — so every day feels safe, and every person feels understood.

Plan a visit

The journey of memory loss affects the whole family. We’re here to be a safe harbor — care that sees the person first, routines that bring calm, and moments that still bring joy.

Our approach

Meeting each person where they are.

We move beyond supervision to a way of life that nourishes the spirit — building bridges between a resident’s past and present.

Validation therapy

We honor each resident’s reality with empathy instead of correction — easing anxiety and building trust.

Sensory stimulation

Music, touch, scent and familiar tasks spark recognition and joy, tapping into long-held memories.

Help with daily living

Gentle support with bathing, dressing and grooming that protects dignity and independence.

Their story, known

We learn former careers, hobbies and family names, turning routine care into genuine connection.

A day with rhythm

Predictable days, peaceful minds.

A steady daily rhythm that mirrors the comforts of home helps reduce the agitation and confusion that can come with cognitive change.

  1. Morning

    Gentle start

    An unhurried wake-up, help getting ready, breakfast, then a morning group circle to set the day.

  2. Midday

    Calm dining

    Home-cooked meals in a quiet, inviting dining room, with gentle encouragement and plenty of hydration.

  3. Afternoon

    Purposeful moments

    Music, familiar tasks and social hour — every activity chosen for a therapeutic reason.

  4. Evening

    Quiet wind-down

    A consistent, soothing evening routine that makes rest come more easily.

Safety & clinical oversight

Watchful care, gently given.

  • A registered nurse in-house, 24/7

    Physicians, nurse practitioners and an RN consultant are part of our interdisciplinary team — and a phone call away.

  • Monthly on-site training

    Every caregiver trains on-site each month in dementia care and medication management.

  • Designed for wayfinding

    Visual cues, clear pathways and secure entries help residents move about with confidence.

  • Reading the unspoken

    Our team is trained to notice non-verbal signs of pain or discomfort, and care plans adapt as needs change.

Levels of care

Care that grows with them.

We provide memory care from baseline to six levels above baseline. As needs change, care changes with them — in the same familiar home, with the same familiar faces.
