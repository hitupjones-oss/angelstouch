About Angels Touch

Since 2008, Angels Touch has been a safe place to call home — three neighboring residences in the Town of Ledgeview, a registered nurse in‑house around the clock, and owners who know every resident personally.

Memory care

What sets us apart is how we care for memory.

Specialized, structured, deeply personal

  • Monthly on-site training in dementia care and medication management for every caregiver.
  • Validation therapy & sensory stimulation that meet residents where they are.
  • Calm, familiar routines and wayfinding cues that build confidence.
  • Physicians, nurse practitioners and an RN consultant on the care team.
