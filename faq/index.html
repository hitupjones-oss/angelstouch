Care & services

We offer a full spectrum of support, from baseline assistance to six levels higher as a resident’s needs change. Services include help with daily living, medication administration, memory care, three meals a day plus snacks and nutritional guidance, specialty diets, and assistance arranging transportation to appointments — all designed to promote dignity, comfort and independence.

Memory care supports residents living with Alzheimer’s disease, dementia and other memory-related conditions. Our caregivers receive monthly on-site training in dementia care, and our environment is designed for safety, familiarity and routine. Learn about our memory care.

Yes. We provide memory care at baseline and up to six levels above baseline, so residents with more advanced dementia can be cared for here — and stay as their needs change.

Yes. Our community is staffed 24/7, with a registered nurse in-house and physicians a phone call away. Physicians, nurse practitioners and an RN consultant are part of our interdisciplinary team.

Every resident begins with a detailed assessment of their needs, preferences and routines. Care plans are reviewed regularly and adjusted as those needs evolve — with families kept in the loop.

Living here

We offer studio and one-bedroom suites, each with a private bathroom and shower, DirecTV, DirecTV music and Wi-Fi. Suites are handicap accessible. Residents are encouraged to bring their own furniture and keepsakes — and if you need furniture, just let us know. See floor plans.

Yes. Our residences have large outdoor patios and recreational space where residents can relax, garden and socialize, designed with accessibility and safety in mind.

A designated activity staff plans music, movies, card and board games, weekly bingo, gentle fitness and sensory programs, plus field trips, the county fair and visiting entertainers. Explore activities.

Our chef and cooks prepare meals from scratch, and our interdisciplinary team reviews menus regularly. We accommodate medical diets, preferences and different textures, and residents always have a say in their dietary choices. More about dining.

We’re a pet-friendly community and understand the comfort a beloved companion can bring. Please talk with us about your pet’s needs.

Costs & payment

We accept check, invoice and ACH payments — with a discount for ACH — and we provide long-term care insurance billing assistance. Call us to talk through costs for your loved one’s level of care.
