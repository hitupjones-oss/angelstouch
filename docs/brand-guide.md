# Angels Touch — Brand guide (2026)

**Essence:** *A safe place to call home.* Thoughtful, elegant and warm — premium without being cold,
vibrant without being loud. Every choice should feel like the care itself: attentive, unhurried, dignified.

---

## Color

The brand color is **Angel Blue** — a confident, trustworthy blue lifted from the original logo and modernized.
It sits on warm, off-white **porcelain** grounds rather than stark white, which keeps the site soft and human.

**Accessibility (WCAG 2.1):** Angel Blue on Porcelain 5.96:1 · White on Angel Blue 6.33:1 · Ink on Porcelain 15.2:1 ·
Slate on Porcelain 6.14:1 · Blue 200 on Midnight 12.25:1 · Gilded light on Midnight 10.7:1 — all pass AA for body text.
Never set Gilded or Sunrise text on light backgrounds.

## Typography

| Role | Typeface | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Display / headlines** | **Fraunces** (variable, optical size 9–144) | A soft, high-contrast serif that reads as excellence and warmth. Set light (300–400) at large sizes; *italic* for the emotional half of a headline. |
| **Body** | **Figtree** (variable) | Friendly, open, highly legible geometric sans for long reading at 17–18px. |
| **Details** | **Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono** | Designed with the Braille Institute for maximum legibility. Used small and uppercase for eyebrows, labels, times, captions and data — fitting for a senior-care brand. |

Headline pattern: a plain statement followed by an italic, blue emotional turn —
“Care that remembers *who they are.*” · “Real food, *made from scratch.*”

All fonts are self-hosted (no third-party requests) via Fontsource.

## Logo

The original script wordmark was vectorized from the source PNG (`public/logo.svg`) so it stays crisp at any size.
The halo is a separate layer, which lets it descend and glow in the title film’s final shot.
Use white on footage/Midnight, Angel Blue on light grounds. Keep clear space of one halo-height on all sides.

## Photography & film

- Close, warm, candid moments: laughter, hands, shared tasks, morning light.
- Creamy highlights, soft blue shadows, gentle grain — consistent with the color system.
- Caregivers wear cornflower-blue scrubs, echoing Angel Blue.
- Real buildings and rooms are always shown with real photos.

## Motion principles

1. **Unhurried.** Long ease-outs (`cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)`), nothing snaps.
2. **Reveal, don’t bounce.** Text rises out of a mask; media unmasks from a rounded frame.
3. **Motion explains.** Counters, drawn floor plans, a filling daily-rhythm line and growing care-level bars carry information.
4. **Always optional.** Every animation respects *Reduce Motion*; the film has pause, skip and chapters.

## Voice

Warm, plain-spoken and specific. Speak to families as neighbors. Prefer “residents” and “your loved one”;
avoid clinical jargon unless it reassures (e.g. “registered nurse in-house, 24/7”).
CTA wording is short and human: **Call Me Back** · **Plan a visit** · **Book a video call**.
