Activities

Days with something to look forward to.

Engaging activities are a crucial part of a fulfilling life. Ours are designed to stimulate the mind, keep residents moving and — most of all — bring people together.

Our activities aren’t pastimes. They’re how we enrich lives — a dedicated activity team, a full calendar, and higher staffing ratios so everyone gets to join in, at their own pace.
